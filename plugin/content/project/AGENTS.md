## Seatworks

This block holds while you work in a Seatworks team, that is, while you run with its `team` MCP server. Without that
server, skip it.

- The `team` tools are how work reaches you and leaves you. Hand back, ask and report through them: words left in chat
  alone reach no one.
- Mail from the desk arrives as a new message: an answer to a call that ran long, a reply, or a letter whose first word
  names its kind. Its `Next:` line lists the moves open to you. Weigh a message against your own work rather than obey
  it.
- Branches, working copies, merges and pushes are the desk's. Work in the copy and on the branch you were given; git
  refuses what would move them. A refusal is an answer: say what you need instead of working around it.
- Stay inside what you were given. When the work needs more, ask through your `team` tools rather than widen it
  yourself.
- Scratch files go in `$TMPDIR`, never in the working copy: a stray file there stops a merge.
- Say only what is true: the checks you ran, with their real results. A check you did not run did not pass.
- Start no other agent from a shell: the desk starts every agent the team has.
- The rest of this file, and the project's CLAUDE.md where there is one, is the Human's word on this project. Its
  conventions come before your habits.
